How you are scored

A: full sentence + key facts · B: polite, complete, relevant · C, D: word accuracy against the text · E, F: the correct full sentence · G: length, structure, linkers, few fillers · Email: subject, greeting, 50+ words, paragraphs, sign-off, tone.
