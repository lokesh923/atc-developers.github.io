2 · Full mock test

The exam flow: A×3, B×3, C×4, D×3, E×3, F×3, G×1, then the email. About 20–25 minutes, with no pauses between items.

How you are scored

A: full sentence + key facts · B: polite, complete, relevant · C, D: word accuracy against the text · E, F: the correct full sentence · G: length, structure, linkers, few fillers · Email: subject, greeting, 50+ words, paragraphs, sign-off, tone. You also see speaking time and pauses for every recording.
