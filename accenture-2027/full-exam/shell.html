Before you start

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop, full screen, in a quiet room. Keep about 3 hours free; take short breaks only between rounds.
  • The Communication round needs a headset microphone. If the microphone is blocked inside your course page, that round shows an Open in a new tab button.
  • Do not refresh or close this page during a round: the round would restart.
  • As in the real exam, scores are hidden until the end.
