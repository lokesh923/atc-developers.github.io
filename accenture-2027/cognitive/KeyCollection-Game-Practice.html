How to play

  • You are the dark tile with the person icon. The grey arrows show where you can move.
  • Move up, down, left or right, one cell at a time: tap a neighbouring cell, use the on-screen arrows, or press ↑↓←→ (or WASD).
  • Walls are hidden. If you bump into one, you stay where you are and the wall turns dark.
  • Walk onto every 🔑 key first. The 🚪 door only opens once all keys are collected.
  • Finish each level before the 4:00 timer runs out, using as few moves as you can.
Winning methodCount the Manhattan distance (rows + columns) to the key. Every move should bring you closer. When you're blocked, try the other direction that still gets closer, then a sidestep. Never bump the same wall twice.
