How to play

  • The rocket (left) must reach the planet (right) along the track. Every tile's arrows show its direction of travel.
  • Tap a tile to select it (yellow box).
  • ⟳ Rotate turns the selected tile 90° clockwise. Tiles have a direction, so a straight tile pointing ← must be rotated twice to point →.
  • ⇄ Swap: select a tile, press Swap, then tap the tile to swap it with. Use this when a tile has the wrong shape.
  • ✓ Check tests your route. If it's valid, the rocket flies to the planet and you move to the next puzzle.
  • When the 4:00 timer ends, the puzzle counts as not solved and the next one starts.

Keyboard: R rotate · S swap · Enter check · Esc cancel swap
