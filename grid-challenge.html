⏳This assessment takes about 9 minutes to complete.

How it works: one dot lights up on the grid; remember where it was. Then decide whether a pattern is symmetrical (the right half mirrors the left half). This repeats. At the end, tap the dots in the order they appeared and press Submit. A fully correct round moves you up a level (one more dot); a mistake moves you down.
