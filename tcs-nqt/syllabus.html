01

NUMERICAL ABILITY

Part A · Foundation
20 Qs · 25 min · ~75 sec each · Moderate
Calculation speed and formula recall decide this section. Expect word problems, some TITA (typed) answers, and mixed-level questions.
Priority is our preparation guide based on how often the topic is practised for NQT: High · Medium · Low. TCS does not publish an official topic weightage.

Number SystemHigh

  • Types of numbers, divisibility rules
  • Unit digit and last two digits of powers
  • Remainders, remainder theorem
  • Factors, number of factors, sum of factors
  • Prime numbers, co-primes
  • Base conversion (binary, octal, hex)
  • Factorial and trailing zeros
  • Recurring decimals, surds and indices

LCM & HCFHigh

  • Prime factorisation method
  • Product of two numbers = LCM x HCF
  • LCM/HCF of fractions
  • Bells ringing together, circular track meeting
  • Greatest number dividing with given remainders
  • Smallest number divisible by a set of numbers

PercentagesHigh

  • Percentage to fraction conversions
  • Successive percentage change
  • Population increase/decrease
  • Percentage in marks, votes, salary problems
  • Consumption vs price change
  • Net change and expenditure problems

Profit & LossHigh

  • CP, SP, MP, markup and discount
  • Successive discounts
  • Dishonest dealer (false weights)
  • Profit on whole vs part
  • Partial sale at different profit/loss
  • Article sold at same SP with gain and loss

Time, Speed & DistanceHigh

  • Average speed, relative speed
  • Trains: crossing poles, platforms, trains
  • Boats and streams (upstream/downstream)
  • Races and circular tracks
  • Meeting and chasing problems
  • Late/early arrival problems

Time & WorkHigh

  • Individual and combined work rate
  • Efficiency and total work method
  • Pipes and cisterns, leakage
  • Alternate-day work
  • Work and wages
  • Men-days-hours (chain rule)

Simple & Compound InterestMedium

  • SI formula, amount and time
  • CI annual, half-yearly, quarterly
  • Difference between CI and SI
  • Population growth as CI
  • Installments (equal EMI)
  • Interest rate and time unknown

AveragesMedium

  • Average of consecutive numbers
  • Weighted average
  • Change in average with addition/removal
  • Age-based averages
  • Average speed and temperature
  • Replacement of members

Ratio, Proportion & PartnershipMedium

  • Ratio simplification, compound ratio
  • Direct and inverse proportion
  • Mean proportional, third proportional
  • Division of amount in ratio
  • Partnership with time and investment
  • Variation (direct, inverse, joint)

ProbabilityMedium

  • Coins, dice, cards
  • Mutually exclusive and independent events
  • At least / at most conditions
  • Balls drawn from a bag
  • Conditional probability (basic)
  • Odds in favour and against

Permutation & CombinationMedium

  • Fundamental counting principle
  • nPr and nCr
  • Arrangements with repetition
  • Circular permutations
  • Word arrangement, rank of a word
  • Selection with restrictions, handshake problems

MensurationMedium

  • Area and perimeter of 2D shapes
  • Volume and surface area of cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere
  • Cost of painting, fencing, flooring
  • Melting and recasting of solids
  • Inscribed and circumscribed figures
  • Water flow in pipes and tanks

AgesLow

  • Present, past and future age equations
  • Ratio of ages
  • Sum and difference of ages
  • Age of family members over years

Mixtures & AlligationMedium

  • Alligation rule
  • Mixing two or more quantities
  • Replacement of liquid (repeated removal)
  • Profit in mixing cheap and costly items
  • Milk and water problems

Other Common TopicsLow

  • Simplification, BODMAS, approximation
  • Square roots, cube roots
  • Equations (linear and quadratic)
  • Series and sequences (AP/GP)
  • Clocks, calendars, coordinate basics

Part A · Foundation
20 Qs · 25 min · ~75 sec each · Moderate
Pattern recognition and structured diagrams. Set up tables and diagrams quickly, since puzzle sets carry multiple questions.
Priority is our preparation guide based on how often the topic is practised for NQT: High · Medium · Low. TCS does not publish an official topic weightage.

Number & Alphabet SeriesHigh

  • Arithmetic, geometric and mixed series
  • Difference and square/cube patterns
  • Alternating and two-step series
  • Missing and wrong term
  • Letter series with positional values
  • Alpha-numeric series

Coding – DecodingHigh

  • Letter shifting and reverse coding
  • Number and symbol coding
  • Word to number mapping
  • Sentence coding
  • Conditional coding
  • Mixed (alphabet + number) coding

Blood RelationsHigh

  • Direct and indirect relations
  • Family tree construction
  • Coded blood relations
  • Pointing to a photograph
  • Generation-based questions
  • Gender-unknown cases

Directions & DistancesHigh

  • Cardinal and ordinal directions
  • Net displacement (Pythagoras)
  • Shadow based directions
  • Turns left/right sequences
  • Shortest distance after movement

Seating ArrangementsHigh

  • Linear (single and double row)
  • Circular (facing inside/outside)
  • Square and rectangular tables
  • Conditional and uncertain placements
  • Floor and box arrangement

SyllogismsHigh

  • Categorical statements (All, Some, No)
  • Venn diagram method
  • Possibility and definite conclusions
  • Either-or and complementary pairs
  • Reverse syllogism

AnalogiesMedium

  • Word analogies (function, type, part)
  • Number analogies
  • Letter analogies
  • Classification analogies

Odd One OutMedium

  • Word, number and letter classification
  • Number pattern odd one

Clock & CalendarMedium

  • Angle between hands

  • Gain and loss of time
  • Day of the week for any date
  • Leap year and odd days

Ranking & OrderingMedium

  • Position from left/right/top/bottom

  • Interchange of positions
  • Comparison-based ordering
  • Age and height ordering

Alphabet & Number TestsMedium

  • Position and rank in alphabet
  • Meaningful words from letters
  • Letter-pair removal and counting
  • Digits and arrangement counting
  • Mirror/reverse alphabet

Symbol Operations & InequalityMedium

  • Redefined operators (@, #, $)
  • Coded inequalities
  • Mathematical operator swapping
  • Conclusion-based inequality

Other Possible TopicsLow

  • Data arrangement and puzzles
  • Statement and assumption/conclusion
  • Cubes and dice (basic)
  • Venn diagrams (basic)

03

VERBAL ABILITY

Part A · Foundation (Updated Pattern 2027)
25 + tasks · 25 min · timed per item
Fully restructured. Three task types, each with a hard per-question timer. Work on speed, memory and email structure.
Priority is our preparation guide based on how often the topic is practised for NQT: High · Medium · Low. TCS does not publish an official topic weightage.

Sentence Completion (20 items, 25 s each)High

  • Grammar and tense usage
  • Subject-verb agreement
  • Articles, prepositions, conjunctions
  • Pronouns and modifiers
  • Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, word choice
  • Idioms and phrases, collocations
  • Error spotting and correct usage
  • Parallelism and sentence structure

Passage Recall (4 + 4 tasks, 30 s read + 90 s write)High

  • Passage disappears after 30 seconds
  • Read for key points, names, numbers, sequence
  • Reconstruct in your own words from memory
  • Keep sentences short and factual
  • Practise chunking: who, what, when, why
  • Typing speed and accuracy matter

Email Writing (1 task, 9 min)High

  • Subject line, greeting, closing
  • Formal tone and clarity
  • Structure: purpose, details, action, thanks
  • Complaint, request, apology, follow-up, information emails
  • Grammar and spelling accuracy
  • Word limit and time management

Instruction ScreensLow

  • Short timed screens before each task
  • Read them quickly, the timer runs
  • Know the format in advance via mocks

Skills Tested OverallMedium

  • Grammar accuracy
  • Vocabulary range
  • Short-term memory
  • Professional written communication
  • Typing speed

Data InterpretationHigh

  • Mixed and caselet DI
  • Percentage, ratio, average from data
  • Growth, CAGR and comparison
  • Approximation techniques

Set Theory & Venn DiagramsHigh

  • Union, intersection formulas
  • Only/exactly/at least counts
  • Survey based questions
  • Maximum and minimum overlap

Data SufficiencyHigh

  • Statement I and II sufficiency
  • Quantitative and logical forms
  • Combined statement analysis
  • Yes/No and value type questions

Syllogisms (Advanced)Medium

  • Multiple statements and conclusions
  • Possibility cases
  • Venn diagram with 3-4 terms

Advanced ProbabilityMedium

  • Conditional probability, Bayes basics
  • With/without replacement
  • Expectation-style questions
  • Geometric and arrangement-based probability

Permutation & Combination (Advanced)High

  • Restricted arrangements
  • Distribution of objects (identical/distinct)
  • Circular and necklace
  • Digits and numbers formation
  • Derangement-style problems

PartnershipMedium

  • Investment x time ratio
  • Entry and exit of partners

Critical ReasoningMedium

  • Assumption, inference, strengthen/weaken
  • Argument evaluation
  • Course of action

Analytical PuzzlesHigh

  • Multi-variable arrangement puzzles
  • Scheduling and ordering puzzles
  • Truth/liar and logic puzzles
  • Grouping and selection puzzles
  • Tournament and ranking puzzles

Advanced Word ProblemsMedium

  • Time-speed-work combined

  • Number system with remainders (advanced)

05

ADVANCED CODING

Part B · Advanced
2 problems · 90 min · Easy ~35 min + Medium ~55 min
Languages: C, C++, Java, Python, Perl. Judged on hidden test cases. Always handle edge cases and mind time complexity.
Priority is our preparation guide based on how often the topic is practised for NQT: High · Medium · Low. TCS does not publish an official topic weightage.

Programming FundamentalsHigh

  • Input/output formats, multiple test cases
  • Loops, conditions, nested logic
  • Functions and parameter passing
  • Data types, overflow, integer division
  • Pattern printing (pyramids, diamonds)
  • Number printing and series generation

ArraysHigh

  • Traversal, min/max, second largest
  • Rotation and reversal
  • Prefix sums and difference arrays
  • Kadane's (max subarray)
  • Duplicates, missing and repeating elements
  • Merging and partitioning
  • Rearrangement (positive/negative, zeros to end)
  • Frequency counting

Matrix / 2D ArraysMedium

  • Row, column and diagonal traversal
  • Spiral and zig-zag traversal
  • Rotation and transpose
  • Saddle point, boundary sum
  • Search in sorted matrix

StringsHigh

  • Palindrome, anagram, pangram
  • Reverse words and sentences
  • Character frequency
  • Substrings and subsequences
  • Compression and run-length encoding
  • Pattern matching, longest common prefix
  • Case conversion, vowels and consonants
  • Parsing and tokenising input

Math & Number TheoryHigh

  • Prime check, sieve of Eratosthenes
  • GCD, LCM, Euclid's algorithm
  • Factorial, Fibonacci, power
  • Perfect, Armstrong, strong numbers
  • Modular arithmetic and fast exponentiation
  • Base conversion
  • Digit manipulation (sum, reverse)
  • Combinatorics basics (nCr)

Sorting & SearchingHigh

  • Bubble, selection, insertion sort
  • Merge and quick sort
  • Binary search and its variants
  • Custom comparator sorting
  • K-th largest/smallest
  • Search in rotated sorted array
  • Counting sort basics

Two Pointers & Sliding WindowMedium

  • Pair sum in sorted array
  • Remove duplicates in place
  • Fixed-size window max/sum
  • Longest substring without repetition
  • Subarray with given sum

Hashing, Sets & MapsMedium

  • Two-sum and variants
  • Group anagrams
  • First non-repeating character
  • Subarray sum equals K
  • Frequency-based problems

Recursion & BacktrackingMedium

  • Factorial, Fibonacci recursion
  • Subsets and permutations
  • N-Queens, Sudoku basics
  • Tower of Hanoi
  • Combination sum
  • Maze/path problems

Stack & QueueMedium

  • Balanced parentheses
  • Next greater/smaller element
  • Expression evaluation (postfix/infix)
  • Queue using stacks
  • Monotonic stack basics

Linked ListLow

  • Reverse, middle element
  • Cycle detection
  • Merge two sorted lists
  • Remove duplicates, delete nth node

Trees & GraphsLow

  • Binary tree traversals
  • Height, diameter, level order
  • BST insert/search
  • BFS and DFS
  • Shortest path and connected components

Greedy & Dynamic ProgrammingHigh

  • Activity selection, minimum coins
  • Knapsack (0/1)
  • Longest increasing/common subsequence
  • Grid paths, climbing stairs
  • Partition and subset sum
  • Edit distance

Bit ManipulationLow

  • Set bit count, parity
  • Power of two check
  • XOR tricks (single number)
  • Swap and toggle bits

Simulation & Real-world ProblemsMedium

  • Date, time and calendar logic
  • Bank, billing, parking style simulations
  • Game and grid movement
  • Scheduling and queue processing

Exam-day Coding StrategyHigh

  • Read constraints, estimate complexity
  • Write brute force first, then optimise
  • Test with edge cases (empty, one element, max size)
  • Handle input format exactly
  • Solve Easy fully before Medium
  • Partial credit counts: pass as many test cases as possible
